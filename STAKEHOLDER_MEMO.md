# Stakeholder Memo — Proposed Enterprise Workflow Investment

**Decision requested:** Approve an 11 team-week scope within a 12 team-week envelope, subject to evidence and sizing validation.

The proposal funds auditability, connector failure recovery, and agent-confirmed routing. These form a coherent operational path: get workflows connected, support ownership decisions, and preserve accountability for those decisions.

We are not funding autonomous routing. The synthetic evaluation demonstrates why queue accuracy alone can conceal urgent escalation failures. Routing assistance therefore requires human confirmation and an audit trail.

The adoption dashboard narrowly misses the baseline allocation. Its score is 26.7 compared with 27.0 for routing. If routing confidence weakens, the dashboard becomes the preferred substitute. This is a reversible sequencing choice, not a judgment that analytics is unimportant.

The budget includes one team-week of contingency. Any request to add a feature must name a displaced item or increase the approved capacity. Engineering estimates and customer evidence must replace the fictional planning inputs before this becomes a real commitment.

**Success review:** Assess connector recovery behavior, confirmed routing quality, urgent escalation handling, and completeness of the decision audit. Review adoption separately from release completion. No business outcome is claimed until measured.
